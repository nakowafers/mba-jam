# Average Lock-In Score for Weekly Contender and Trade Valuation

Raw MBA fantasy points per game (`FP/G`) systematically understates weekly scoring in a Sleeper NBA Lock-In league (`game_mode: 1`), where Managers lock a player's best single game from 2–4 weekly outings. We derive each player's **Average Lock-In Score** from completed Sleeper matchup legs (`1 .. currentLeg - 1`) across all non-zero rostered weeks (Starter Slot and Bench, excluding `0`-point DNP weeks while retaining negative scores, and falling back to `1.0x FP/G` when a player has zero completed weeks) so every Roster shares a stable, jitter-free completed-week window. **Starting 10 Avg Lock-In** drives Power Rankings **Contender Score** (recalibrating the contender ceiling from `340` to `400`) and supersedes raw `FP/G` on the Starting 10 Δ axis of ADR-0002's trade model, while core **Dynasty Value** remains anchored to raw/blended `FP/G` to preserve multi-year, sample-size-stable asset pricing.
