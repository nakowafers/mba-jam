# Trade Grade Calibration, Pick-Offset Waiver Baseline, and Settlement Transfers

Evaluating live and historical MBA trades under ADR-0002 through ADR-0004 exposed severe grade polarization and mutual-loss artifacts when raw bench **Dynasty Values** (`~20–28`, which include an unsubtracted waiver floor) were deducted in full against net-priced **Draft Pick Assets**, when open **Roster** slots were ignored, and when pure Axis 1 **Dynasty Value** spreads judged cross-timeline contender/rebuilder deals and unilateral transfers. We refine the dual-axis trade model with five domain rules: (1) open active (`< 15`) and matching open `IR` (`Out`) slots absorb excess incoming players at zero cost in `LIVE` mode (while `HISTORICAL` hindsight re-grades assume deterministic `15`-slot capacity and `Mid` anchors for completed `2025` picks); (2) forced **Replacement Cut Assets** cap individual bench drops at `24.0` and apply a `14.0` **Waiver Replacement Baseline** credit strictly to net outgoing **Draft Pick Asset** conversions while retaining `100%` cut penalties on multi-player dumps (`2-for-1`, `3-for-1`); (3) **Consolidation Premium** follows a continuous cornerstone curve above `58.0` **Dynasty Value** and weights uneven consolidation by extra players and first-round picks only; (4) **Starting 10 Avg Lock-In** (`Axis 2`) dampens losses (`0.65x`) when acquiring picks, youth, or sidelined `0-GP`/`Out` cornerstones and grants a `2.2x` win-now boost (`1.5x` base; `1.65x` neutral in `HISTORICAL`) to **Rosters** ranking top-five in optimal **Starting 10 Avg Lock-In** either pre- or post-trade, pairing with asset-anchored **Trade Grade** bands (`±5.5` `B`, `+32` `A+`, `-48` `F`) and timeline-aware **Trade Fairness**; and (5) any proposal or historical transaction where a participating **Roster** has zero incoming or zero outgoing assets resolves atomically to `N/A` (**Settlement Transfer** / incomplete proposal) with no winner while still propagating **Flipped Asset** chains.
